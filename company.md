## 5. Membership Policies & Guidelines

### 5.1 Enrollment & Onboarding Process
1. **Intake Registration:** Completion of official enrollment form via web portal or front desk concierge.
2. **Health Readiness Screening:** Completion of mandatory PAR-Q+ (Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire) and liability waiver.
3. **Athletic Baseline Orientation:** Optional complimentary 30-minute facility walkthrough and biometric scan with a staff coach.
4. **Digital Credentialing:** Digital keycard issued directly inside the Apple Wallet / Google Wallet via FORGE App.

### 5.2 Contract Length & Terms
- **Month-to-Month:** Auto-renews monthly until 30-day written cancellation notice is received.
- **Annual Agreement:** 12-month commitment offering a 20% discount on monthly dues. Rolls into standard month-to-month billing at the discounted loyalty rate upon completion of the initial 12-month term.

### 5.3 Membership Freeze Policy
- Members in good standing may place their account on temporary administrative freeze for **up to 60 total calendar days per calendar year** (minimum freeze window: 14 consecutive days).
- **Eligible Freeze Reasons:** Medical injury or illness (with physician note), documented corporate relocation/travel, or military deployment.
- **Freeze Fee:** $15 / month maintenance fee for non-medical leaves; waived completely upon presentation of medical verification.
- **Notice Period:** Freeze requests must be submitted at least 7 business days prior to the desired freeze start date.

### 5.4 Guest Privilege Policy
- Guests must be at least 18 years of age (or 16 accompanied by parent/legal guardian).
- Guests must complete a digital safety waiver and present a valid government-issued photo ID prior to floor entry.
- A single individual may visit as a guest of any member a maximum of twice per calendar year before being required to purchase a day pass or membership.

### 5.5 Age Requirements
- **18+ Years Old:** Full independent access to all facilities and equipment.
- **16 – 17 Years Old:** Permitted with signed parental/guardian consent on file; permitted during staffed operating hours.
- **Under 16 Years Old:** Permitted exclusively when enrolled in supervised Youth Athletic Development clinics or private coaching sessions.

## 8. Rules & Athlete Code of Conduct

To preserve an environment of high focus, safety, and mutual respect, all members and guests must strictly adhere to the following standards:

### 8.1 Floor Etiquette
1. **Re-Racking Obligation:** Every barbell, dumbbell, kettlebell, bumper plate, and attachment must be returned to its designated storage rack immediately after use. Leaving loaded bars or loose plates on the floor is grounds for membership review.
2. **Sanitization Protocol:** Every athlete must thoroughly wipe down benches, bars, handles, and machine surfaces with provided antimicrobial hospital-grade wipes after every exercise bout.
3. **Platform Safety Zones:** Maintain at least a 6-foot safety radius around active Olympic lifting platforms. Never cross in front of, behind, or across a platform while an athlete is performing a lift.
4. **Dropping Weights:** Bumper plates may be dropped from overhead or waist height *only* on designated wood-and-rubber Olympic platforms. Dumbbells, kettlebells, and steel plates must never be dropped intentionally on gym rubber floors or turf.
5. **Chalk Usage:** Pure magnesium gym chalk is provided in chalk bins across Zones 01 and 02. Keep hands inside chalk bowls while applying. Clapping chalk into ambient air is strictly prohibited.

### 8.2 Dress Code & Apparel
- **Footwear:** Clean, closed-toe athletic training shoes, Olympic weightlifting shoes, or dedicated minimalist/barefoot shoes are required in all zones. Socks or bare feet are permissible *only* on competition lifting platforms during deadlifts and on calisthenics mats during bodyweight acrobatics. Crocs, open sandals, slides, or muddy street footwear are strictly banned.
- **Clothing:** High-performance athletic apparel (shorts, athletic tights, compression gear, sports bras, t-shirts, tank tops) must be worn. Clothing with abrasive exposed zippers, buttons, or metal rivets that damage leather upholstery is prohibited.

### 8.3 Photography, Video & Device Etiquette
- **Content Creation:** Form-check video recording is permitted using personal mobile phones provided the tripod or phone does not block thoroughfares or other athletes' training areas.
- **Privacy Standard:** Photography and video recording of any kind inside locker rooms, steam rooms, saunas, and cryotherapy suites is **strictly prohibited under penalty of immediate membership revocation and legal referral**.
- **Audio:** Personal audio devices must be used with headphones or earbuds. Playing speaker audio aloud on the gym floor is not allowed.

## 11. Health, Safety & Facility Hygiene Policies

### 11.1 Air Quality & Climate Systems
- Full-facility industrial **HEPA-13 air filtration systems** cycling indoor air volume 8 times per hour.
- Dual in-duct commercial **UV-C germicidal irradiation arrays** neutralizing airborne pathogens.
- Climate control maintained strictly at 20°C (68°F) with 45% relative humidity for optimal physical output and chalk stabilization.

### 11.2 Daily Sanitization Regimens
- **Athlete Self-Sanitization:** Over 30 touch-free disinfectant wipe dispensers and hand-sanitizer stations positioned every 20 feet throughout the floor.
- **Hourly Sweeps:** Operations staff perform dedicated contact-surface disinfection on dumbbells, handles, and touchscreens every 60 minutes.
- **Electrostatic Fogging:** Nightly electrostatic hospital-grade antimicrobial fogging across all 28,000 square feet.

### 11.3 Equipment Maintenance & Rig Inspections
- **Olympic Barbells:** Cleaned, degreased, and oiled weekly; sleeve bearings and bushings inspected for angular runout bi-weekly.
- **Calisthenics Rig & Rings:** High-tensile climbing-grade straps and birch wooden rings inspected daily at 05:00 for wear, splintering, or fraying. Load-bearing hardware torqued to manufacturer specs weekly.
- **Emergency Defibrillator (AED) & Medical Kits:** 3 automated external defibrillators (AEDs) deployed across Zone 01, Zone 04, and Front Lobby. All full-time coaches and desk staff hold active American Red Cross Adult CPR/AED and First Aid certifications.

## 12. Cancellation & Refund Policy

### 12.1 Cooling-Off Period (Statutory Right of Cancellation)
- New members enjoy an unconditional **3-business-day (72-hour) cooling-off period** from initial contract signature.
- Cancellations requested within this window receive a 100% full refund of all enrollment fees and dues paid, minus the cost of any retail items or consumable services already utilized.

### 12.2 Standard Cancellation Terms
- **30-Day Written Notice:** Members on month-to-month contracts may cancel at any time by providing written notice at least 30 calendar days prior to their next scheduled billing date.
- **Submission Method:** Cancellation requests must be submitted through the official online member cancellation portal or via signed certified letter sent to `billing@forgeathletics.com`. Verbal, phone, or direct-message requests cannot be processed.
- **Final Dues Payment:** If a billing date falls within the 30-day notice window, the final monthly dues will be charged, and full facility access remains active through the end of that final paid period.

### 12.3 Annual Contract Early Termination
- Members enrolled in a 12-month annual commitment may terminate early under the following qualifying conditions without penalty:
  1. **Medical Disability / Permanent Incapacity:** Certified in writing by an attending licensed physician prohibiting physical exercise for 90+ days.
  2. **Permanent Relocation:** Documented permanent relocation to a primary residence greater than 25 miles from any operating FORGE facility (utility bill or lease agreement required).
- Early cancellation for non-qualifying personal preference prior to the 12-month expiration incurs an **Early Termination Buyout Fee of $150** or 50% of the remaining contract value (whichever is lower).

### 12.4 Refund Rules for Special Services
- **Private Coaching Packages:** Unused training sessions are eligible for a 100% refund within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, unused sessions convert to store credit or may be transferred to another member.
- **Retail & Apparel:** Unworn, unwashed merchandise with original tags attached may be returned or exchanged within 14 days of purchase.
- **Consumable & Nutrition Items:** All sales are final on open supplements, bottled beverages, or food items.
